Glass style directions

The same three things in each: sidebar, one opportunity card, and part of the people network. Choose one, or mix parts.

A. Aurora

Dark, bright colour glows behind heavy frosted glass. Most eye-catching for a demo; text can be harder to read.

B. Liquid

Light, clear glass with highlighted edges, close to Apple's Liquid Glass. Feels native to the Mac; the calmest of the three.

C. Obsidian

Near-black smoked glass, one sharp lime accent, crisp thin borders. Fits a lot of information on screen, like Linear or Raycast.
